# Emergency Procedures

## Immediate Safety

Safety-related incidents take priority over schedule and cost optimization.

When an operational condition creates an immediate safety risk, do not continue normal execution until the condition is assessed.

## Crowding

When observed occupancy exceeds room capacity:

1. confirm the occupancy signal
2. identify rooms with sufficient capacity
3. check schedule conflicts
4. propose relocation or crowd-management actions
5. obtain human approval before changing the event plan

## Escalation

High-severity incidents should receive priority over lower-severity operational issues.

When several incidents affect the same event, consider their combined operational impact rather than treating each incident independently.
