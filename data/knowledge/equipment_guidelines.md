# Equipment Guidelines

## Equipment Availability

Equipment should be considered available only when:

- equipment status is AVAILABLE
- sufficient quantity exists for the requested assignment
- the equipment is not known to be failed or offline

## Equipment Failure

Equipment reported as OFFLINE must not be relied upon for an event until its operational status has been verified.

## Projectors

Events using visual presentations should have access to a working projector.

A failed primary projector should trigger an investigation of available replacement equipment or alternative operational plans.

## Robotics Equipment

Robotics events may require multiple robotics kits.

The required quantity should be checked against currently available inventory before committing an equipment assignment.
