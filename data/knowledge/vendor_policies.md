# Vendor Policies

## Vendor Availability

A vendor must have ACTIVE status before being assigned to an event.

A vendor is unavailable when it has an overlapping active event assignment.

## Vendor Cancellation

When an assigned vendor cancels, NexCord should:

1. identify the affected event
2. determine the operational impact
3. identify replacement options
4. compare cost and delay
5. obtain human approval before executing the selected response

## Audio Visual Vendors

Audio visual vendors should be checked before events that depend on projectors, displays, or PA equipment.

A vendor marked ACTIVE is not automatically available for every event; schedule overlap must also be checked.
